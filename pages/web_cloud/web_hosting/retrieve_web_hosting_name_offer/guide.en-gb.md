## Objective

You have forgotten the name of your [web hosting offer](/links/web/hosting)?
You want to check its details or compare it to another offer?

During the use of your services, you may need the name of your web hosting offer.

**Find out how to identify the type of web hosting offer you have from your OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- Have a [web hosting offer](/links/web/hosting).

<!-- CP-NAV-START:web-hosting -->
---

### OVHcloud Control Panel Access

- **Direct link:** [Hosting plans](/links/control-panel/web-hosting)
- **Navigation path:** `Web Cloud`{.action} > `Hosting plans`{.action} > Select your web hosting plan

---
<!-- CP-NAV-END:web-hosting -->

## Instructions

<!-- CP-STEPS-START:find-plan-name -->
Click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

> [!tabs]
> **Step 1**
>>
>> Go to the [Hosting plans](/links/control-panel/web-hosting) page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.
>>
>> ![Web Hosting](/pages/assets/screens/control_panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png){.thumbnail}
>>
> **Step 2**
>>
>> In the **Plan** section, you will find the mention **Service plan**.
>>
>> ![Plan](/pages/assets/screens/control_panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/find-plan-pro.png){.thumbnail}
>>
>> Under this mention, you will find the name of your web hosting offer (e.g., Personal, Pro, Performance, etc.).
<!-- CP-STEPS-END:find-plan-name -->
