## Objective

Upon logging-in, you will arrive at the [Platform Home](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/homepage) (or Homepage). Furthermore, from any page in the platform (including the Home) you have access to the two main ways to quickly move around: the [Sidebar](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/sidebar)
 and the [Header](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/header).

## Homepage

 This is the central hub of the platform where you can see all your [Projects](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/project/00-project-index) and easily go anywhere in the platform.

[Learn more about the Home](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/homepage)

## Sidebar

The sidebar allows you to **navigate per service**, i.e. select which service you want to go to and than the Project you want to open it in. You can also find your [Favorites](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/sidebar#favorites) in the Sidebar.

[Learn more about the Sidebar](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/sidebar)

## Header

The Header on the other hand allows you to **navigate per Project**, i.e. select which Project you want to go to and than the service you want to open within the Project.

[Learn more about the Header](/pages/public_cloud/data_platform/product/navigation/header)
