## Objective

**This guide details how to associate an OVHcloud identity (a user) with a global IAM policy**.

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud account](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-creation/).
- One or more Hosted Private Cloud products - VMware on OVHcloud linked to this account (Hosted Private Cloud powered by VMware, VMware Service Pack).
- IAM enabled for your Hosted Private Cloud service - VMware on OVHcloud. Follow the steps in the guide [IAM for VMware on OVHcloud - How to enable IAM](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-activation/).

## Instructions

### How do I associate a user with a global IAM policy?

Log in to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.pl/&ovhSubsidiary=pl). Click on your name in the top right-hand corner of the OVHcloud Control Panel, then click on your initials to go to the <code className="action">My account</code> section.

Under <code className="action">My account</code>, click <code className="action">Identities and Access Management (IAM)</code> and stay in the <code className="action">Policies</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="IAM USER POLICY" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-user-policy/iam_policies_resize.png" loading="lazy" />

You will find here the policies you have already created.

Click your policy or click the <code className="action">...</code> then <code className="action">Modify policy</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="IAM USER POLICY" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-user-policy/iam_policies_modify.png" loading="lazy" />

Then enter the identity you want in the `Local users` and `User groups` boxes, by clicking <code className="action">Add users</code> or <code className="action">Add user groups</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="IAM USER POLICY" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-user-policy/iam_add_user.png" loading="lazy" />

Only IAM Groups (not vSphere IAM Groups) appear automatically, so make sure you copy/paste your choice without errors.

Confirm the addition of your user by clicking <code className="action">Add</code>.

Then, to finish your policy, click <code className="action">Modify policy</code>.

:::info
If your identity is not present, you will need to add it first in the OVHcloud Control Panel.

:::
