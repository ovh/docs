## Objective

Learn how to adjust the flavor of your cluster according to your needs.

## How to upgrade your flavor

This option is available through the OVHcloud [API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/) and the [Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.com/fr/&ovhSubsidiary=fr).

### Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.com/fr/&ovhSubsidiary=fr) or to the [API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

### Instructions

To adjust the flavor of your cluster, log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.com/fr/&ovhSubsidiary=fr) and open your Public Cloud project. Click <code className="action">Data Analysis</code> or <code className="action">Data Streaming</code> in the left navigation bar, then select your engine instance.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Cluster overview" src="/images/public-cloud/data-analytics/analytics/analytics-update-cluster-flavor/cluster_overview.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Change node template</code> and adjust the flavor of your cluster.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Change node template" src="/images/public-cloud/data-analytics/analytics/analytics-update-cluster-flavor/flavor_change.png" loading="lazy" />

## How to downgrade your flavor

This option is currently available via the OVHcloud [API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/).

### Requirements

- Access to the OVHcloud [API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/) (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps/))
- A [Public Cloud project](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

### Instructions

In order to be able to downgrade your flavor, you must have a threshold of 20% between your current disk space used (base storage + additional storage) and the maximum storage space allowed for the flavor.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Change node template" src="/images/public-cloud/data-analytics/analytics/analytics-update-cluster-flavor/flavor_change.png" loading="lazy" />

For instance, if you have an OpenSearch cluster with db1-15 as flavor and less than 384 GB (80% of 480GB) of disk space used, you can decrease the flavor to db1-7 on that endpoint:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/opensearch/\{clusterId\}"} />
