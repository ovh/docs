## Objective

The OVHcloud Managed Kubernetes service provides you Kubernetes clusters without the hassle of installing or operating them. In this guide we cover how you can easily upgrade your cluster to the next minor version of Kubernetes.

On OVHcloud Managed Kubernetes we have deployed a *Upgrade to the next minor version of Kubernetes* feature to upgrade your cluster from one version to the next (*e.g.* 1.26 to 1.27).

## Requirements 

- An OVHcloud Managed Kubernetes cluster

## Important information on the upgrading process

Before you launch the upgrade, please back up your cluster’s configuration, and check that your worker tools are compatible with this new version.

The upgrading process will try to do a [rolling upgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_release). Please ensure that your cluster includes enough worker nodes to allow for a rolling upgrade. 

To minimise downtime for your pods and your overall service, we will drain each of your nodes, only moving on to the next node when the current one is functional (i.e. when the status changes to ‘Ready’). Your admin components and server API will also be updated.

![Node rolling upgrade - step 1 and 2](/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/node-rolling-upgrade1.png)
![Node rolling upgrade - step 3](/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/node-rolling-upgrade2.png)

This operation usually takes around 5 minutes per node in your cluster, but that can vary depending on your cluster type and size.

Once the update has been performed, you cannot revert to the previous version.

## Instructions

### Step 1 - Ask for a minor version upgrade on the OVH Cloud Manager 

1. Access the administration UI for your OVHcloud Managed Kubernetes clusters by clicking on <code className="action">Managed Kubernetes Service</code> in the left-hand menu.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Access to the administration UI" src="/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/upgrading-kubernetes-version-000.png" loading="lazy" />

1. Click on the right end button of your Kubernetes cluster and choose <code className="action">Manage cluster</code>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Click on the right end button and choose Manage cluster" src="/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/upgrading-kubernetes-version-001.png" loading="lazy" />

1. On the *Management* section, click on <code className="action">Upgrade to the next minor version</code>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Click on Upgrade to the next minor version" src="/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/upgrading-kubernetes-version-002.png" loading="lazy" />

1. Click on <code className="action">Confirm</code>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Click on Confirm" src="/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/upgrading-kubernetes-version-003.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 2 - Wait for the upgrading to end 

The upgrading process can take several minutes (around 5 minutes per node in your cluster). During that time, a message on the OVHcloud Control Panel warns you that the cluster is being upgraded:

  <img className="thumbnail" alt="Wait for the end of the upgrade" src="/images/public-cloud/containers-orchestration/managed-kubernetes/upgrading-kubernetes-version/upgrading-kubernetes-version-004.png" loading="lazy" />
