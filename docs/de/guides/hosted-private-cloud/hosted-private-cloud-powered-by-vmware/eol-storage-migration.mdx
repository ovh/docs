## Objective

Some storages will no longer be available at the end of March 2023 as they will no longer be maintained, as described in this guide: [Life cycle of VMware on OVHcloud](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/lifecycle-policy#datastores-stockage).

We will go through all the steps to migrate your data to more recent storage and disable your obsolete storage.

## Requirements

- Being an administrative contact of your [Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/products/hosted-private-cloud/) to receive login credentials.
- A user account with access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](/links/manager).
- A user account with access to vSphere.

## Instructions

### Adding storage

Using the new storage ranges will provide you with full-SSD (vs SSD-accelerated) disks, 2x2x25Gbps network cards (vs 2x10Gbps), higher availability and resilience. Click this link for more information: [NFS Datastore](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/products/hosted-private-cloud/datastores-nfs/).

Use this guide to add more storage if you have not yet subscribed to a more recent solution: [How do I add storage space?](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/how-to-add-storage)

### Migrating your virtual machines

Go to your vCenter interface and check the virtual machines that are on each of your outdated datastores.

Click on the `storage` icon, select your Datastore in `Shared Storage` to the left. Click on the `VM` tab and stay on `Virtual Machines` to display the virtual machines on this obsolete storage.

![01 check existing VM on datastore 01](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/01-check-existing-vm-on-datastore01.png)

Right-click each of the virtual machines that are on this datastore, choose `Migrate` and use the [VMware Storage vMotion](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/storage-vmotion#finalising-the-operation) guide to migrate to a more recent datastore.

![01 check existing VM on datastore 02](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/01-check-existing-vm-on-datastore02.png)

When the tab no longer contains virtual machines, go to `VM template in folders` to view the saved templates on your *datastore*.

![02 unregister and move template 01](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/02-unregister-and-move-template01.png)

Right-click each of the templates saved in your *datastore* and click `Remove from Inventory`.

> [!warning]
> The template is deleted from the inventory but is still in the *datastore*. You can retrieve it and move it to another *datastore* or delete it if you no longer need it.
>

![02 unregister and move template 02](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/02-unregister-and-move-template02.png)

The templates list is empty, you can keep moving your templates to another datastore.

![02 unregister and move template 03](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/02-unregister-and-move-template03.png)

Go to the `Files` tab, go to a folder in a template and click `Move to`.

![02 unregister and move template 04](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/02-unregister-and-move-template04.png)

Choose a Datastore and click `OK`.

![02 unregister and move template 05](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/02-unregister-and-move-template05.png)

Wait for the task to complete.

![02 unregister and move template 06](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/02-unregister-and-move-template06.png)

Select the destination datastore for the template on the left, go to the Template folder, select the template file and click `Save VM`.

![03 register template 01](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/03-register-template01.png)

Click `NEXT`.

![03 register template 02](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/03-register-template02.png)

Click `NEXT` again.

![03 register template 03](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/03-register-template03.png)

Click `FINISH`.

![03 register template 04](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/03-register-template04.png)

Go to the `VM & templates` tab to make your model appear.

![03 register template 05](/images/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/eol-storage-migration/03-register-template05.png)

### Deleting storage for your VMware cluster

Use this guide to delete your obsolete storage: [Delete a datastore](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/delete-datastore)

> [!warning]
> After deleting your Datastore, log in to your OVHcloud Control Panel to stop subscribing to this service.
