## Instructions

### 1: Check the validity of your domain name subscription

:::warning
You are solely responsible for renewing your web services.<br/>
As a hosting provider, OVHcloud is required to permanently delete any web services (domain names, hosting plans, email accounts, etc.) that have not been renewed in time, as well as all of the data they store.

As a result, we strongly recommend that you enable [automatic renewal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-use-automatic-renewal#instructions/) for all of your OVHcloud subscriptions.

:::


To ensure that your domain name subscription is valid, click on your name at the top right-hand corner of your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB), then click on <code className="action">My offers and services</code> within the right-hand menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="control-panel" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/right-menu/my-solutions-and-services.png" loading="lazy" />|

Renew your domain if necessary by clicking on the <code className="action">...</code> button, then select <code className="action">Renew service</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="renew-service-button" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/order/renew-service-button.png" loading="lazy" />

After the renewal of your offer is completed, your website will be available within a maximum of 48 hours.

### 2: Check the DNS servers

To check that your [DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit/) are valid, click on <code className="action">Domain names</code> in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB), then on your domain name.

#### Scenario 1: No anomalies appear on the DNS servers

Check the servers listed in the <code className="action">DNS servers</code> tab:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="srv-dns-ok2" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-servers/name-dns-server.png" loading="lazy" />

If they are identical to the targets of the `NS` type entries in the <code className="action">DNS zone</code>, go to [step 3](#step3):

<img className="thumbnail" alt="srv-dns-ok" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/dashboard-entry-ns.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Scenario 2: A warning appears above the DNS zone

A warning in the <code className="action">DNS zone</code> tab indicates that the DNS servers used by your domain name are not the ones indicated in your [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit/). Two scenarios are possible:

- Under the sentence "You currently use the following DNS servers", the servers listed are "ns **?** .ovh.net" and "dns **?** .ovh.net" (in which "**?**" stands for a double-digit number):

<img className="thumbnail" alt="warning_other_ovh_dns_srv" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/message-other-ovh-dns-servers.png" loading="lazy" />

Modify the DNS servers as described in [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit/), so that they are identical to the targets of the `NS` type records in your <code className="action">DNS zone</code>.

Your website will then be available within a maximum of 48 hours.

- Under the sentence "You currently use the following DNS servers", the servers listed are not "ns **?** .ovh.net" and "dns **?** .ovh.net".

<img className="thumbnail" alt="warning_external_dns_srv" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/message-external-dns-servers.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
In this situation, please contact your DNS Zone provider, your webmaster or a [OVHcloud partner](https://partner.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/directory/) before making any changes.

The DNS servers used by your domain name may be functional and the problem accessing your website be linked to a missing or incorrect entry in the active [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-general-information/). Changing the DNS servers in this situation might make your email addresses or other online applications related to your domain name unavailable.

:::


#### Scenario 3: No NS-type entries appear in the DNS zone

Your domain’s <code className="action">DNS zone</code> does not contain any `NS` record:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="srv_dns_missing" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/dashboard-entry-ns-missing.png" loading="lazy" />

Back up the current zone by clicking on the <code className="action">Change in text format</code> button:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="change_DNS_zone_change_text_format" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/change-in-text-format.png" loading="lazy" />

Then copy and paste the content of your <code className="action">DNS zone</code> into a text document on your computer.

Then click on <code className="action">Reset my DNS zone</code> and select <code className="action">No, but I want to reset my DNS zone</code>. Select your email and hosting servers and click on <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="change_DNS_zone_reset" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/reset-my-dns-zone.png" loading="lazy" />

Your website will then be available again within a maximum of 24 hours.

### 3: Check the DNS zone <a name="step3"></a>

In this step, you will find your hosting plan’s IP address, then add it to your <code className="action">DNS zone</code>.

If your site is not hosted on the OVHcloud infrastructure or is managed by another provider, please contact the concerned support service.

If your site is hosted on one of our [Web Hosting offers](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/), click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the [Hosting plans](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/web/hosting) page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **General information** box, you will see the addresses under **IPv4** and **IPv6**.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="IPv4-IPv6" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/find-ipv4-and-ipv6.png" loading="lazy" />

In the <code className="action">General information</code> tab, copy the IPv4 and/or IPv6 address of your domain name.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


Then refer to it in your domain’s [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit/), by modifying or creating one or more `A` entries.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="ipv4-DNSzone" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/dashboard-entry-a.png" loading="lazy" />

Your website will then be available within a maximum of 24 hours.
