## Objective

This guide will show you how to view the statistics and logs generated by the [CDN option](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cdn-how-to-use-cdn/) available with your [web hosting](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/) plan.

**Find out how to view your website's statistics and CDN logs with your web hosting plan.**

## Requirements

- A [web hosting plan](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/) with the CDN option enabled on at least one domain name.

- **Direct link:** [Hosting plans](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/web/hosting)
- **Navigation path:** <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> > <code className="action">Hosting plans</code> > Select your web hosting plan

## Instructions

### Viewing CDN statistics

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the [Hosting plans](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/web/hosting) page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="statistics-and-logs" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs.png" loading="lazy" />

In the `Website visit statistics` section, click the <code className="action">View statistics</code> button.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Statistics and logs" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    You are redirected to the tool **OVHcloud Web Statistics**. In this tool, click on the form labelled <code className="action">Select domain</code> located at the top of the page, then choose a domain name for which the CDN is active.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    In the left column, click on the <code className="action">Cache</code> tab.
    
    You will now see the CDN statistics for your domain name.
    
    If you need further information, please refer to our guide on "[Web hosting - View website statistics and logs](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/logs-and-statistics/)".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


### View the CDN logs

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the [Hosting plans](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/web/hosting) page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="statistics-and-logs" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs.png" loading="lazy" />

In the `Website logs` section, click the <code className="action">View logs</code> button.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="View logs" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/view-logs.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    On the new page that pops up, click the <code className="action">CDN</code> button in the banner at the top of the page.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="OSL CDN" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/logs/osl-cdn.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    On the new page that appears:
    
    - Select the domain or subdomain concerned via the <code className="action">Subdomain</code> dropdown menu.
    - Enter the date for which you want to view the CDN logs in the <code className="action">Date</code> field.
    - Then click the <code className="action">Generate</code> button.
    
    :::info

    :::

    
    > CDN logs are unavailable the same day they are generated, so you will need to wait for the next day to view them.
    
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="OSL CDN - Logs generated" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/logs/osl-cdn-subdomain-date-selection.png" loading="lazy" />

In the table that pops up, click the <code className="action">Download</code> button to the right of the domain or subdomain concerned to retrieve the CDN logs.
    
    If you need further information, please refer to our guide on "[Web hosting - View website statistics and logs](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/logs-and-statistics/)".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
