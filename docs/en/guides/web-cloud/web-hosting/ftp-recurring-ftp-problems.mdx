## Instructions

### "This server does not support FTP on TLS" (FileZilla)

<img className="thumbnail" alt="doesnt-support-ftp-on-tls" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/filezilla/doesnt-support-ftp-on-tls.png" loading="lazy" />

This message coming from [FileZilla](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-filezilla-user-guide/) indicates that you have not enabled the SFTP or SSH option from your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB). As a result, the information exchanged between your OVHcloud hosting server and your computer will not be encrypted.

If the data you want to exchange via this link is not confidential, click <code className="action">OK</code>.

Otherwise, go to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB), go to the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> section, then <code className="action">Hosting plans</code>. Select the hosting plan concerned, then choose the <code className="action">FTP-SSH</code> tab.

If you have a [Personal](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/personal-offer/) Web Hosting plan, tick the <code className="action">Disabled</code> box in the <code className="action">SFTP</code> column, then wait a few minutes.

If you have a [Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/professional-offer/) or [Performance](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/performance-offer/) Web Hosting plan, click on the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the FTP user concerned, then on <code className="action">Edit</code>.

Choose <code className="action">SFTP</code> or <code className="action">Enabled</code> (to enable SSH on your hosting), click <code className="action">Next</code> and then click on <code className="action">Confirm</code>. Wait a few minutes.

:::info
For any other error messages, see the `Troubleshooting` section of our [Web Hosting guides](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/overview/).

:::


### I transferred my files with FTP software, but my website does not appear.

First of all, check that your website’s files and folders are in the [root folder](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/hosting-how-to-get-my-website-online#3-upload-the-files-to-the-storage-space/) of your Web Hosting plan.

If you have made a change to your [DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit/) or [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit/) less than 48 hours ago, please wait and reboot your devices regularly to clear their cache.

### My FTP credentials are not working.

If you are unable to log in, change your FTP password as described in this [guide](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-change-password/).

### I encounter random errors on my website.

A lack of file storage capacity on your Web Hosting plan may cause your site to malfunction when you try to modify or update it.

### I can't transfer my files to the FTP server.

Check that your FTP software is connected in "Passive mode" (Configuration mode of an FTP server in which the server determines the connection port).

For example, for [Filezilla](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-filezilla-user-guide/), click on <code className="action">Edit</code>, then on <code className="action">Settings</code> and open the <code className="action">Connection</code> settings. Click on <code className="action">FTP</code> and then choose <code className="action">Passive (recommended)</code>.

Also limit the size of your data transfers (you cannot send more than **5000 files and folders** on OVHcloud shared servers on a single transfer). Split your imports if necessary and use compressed folders.

If you have a [Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/professional-offer/) or [Performance](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/performance-offer/) Web Hosting plan, use the [SSH protocol](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssh-on-webhosting/) to import files on to your file storage space.

### I can't delete the symbolic link "index.html" on my FTP space.

This link is installed by default on OVHcloud Web Hosting plans. It displays this image on your web browser:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="site-under-construction" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/site-under-construction.png" loading="lazy" />

If you have not used the [1-click module](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cms-install-1-click-modules/) feature to create your website, you will need to [log in to the FTP storage space](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-connection/) on your web hosting plan in order to delete the “Website under construction” page manually.
