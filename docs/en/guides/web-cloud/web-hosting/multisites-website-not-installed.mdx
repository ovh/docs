## Objective

You may see the error page “**Site not installed**” displayed on your web browser, particularly when your website is first installed.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="website not installed" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/site-not-installed.png" loading="lazy" />

**This guide explains how to identify and resolve the "Site not installed" error page.**

:::warning
OVHcloud provides services for which you are responsible with regard to their configuration and management. It is therefore your responsibility to ensure that they function correctly.

This guide is designed to help you with common tasks. Nevertheless, we recommend contacting a [specialist provider](https://partner.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/directory/) or reach out to the [OVHcloud community](https://community.ovhcloud.com/community/en) if you encounter any difficulties. We will not be able to assist you. You can find more information in the [Go further](#go-further) section of this guide.

:::


## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud web hosting plan](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/)
- Administrative access to the [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit/) your domain name is attached to.

## Instructions

The “**Site not installed**” page appears for two reasons:

- 1: [Your domain name or subdomain is not declared correctly on your web hosting plan](#check-multisites).
- 2: [Your domain name is not linked to the IP address of your web hosting plan.](#check-dns-domain).

The following steps will allow you to correct the “Site not installed” error in both cases.

### 1 - Verify the declaration of your domain name or subdomain on your web hosting plan <a name="check-multisites"></a>

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps in succession.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB), then go to the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> section.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Cloud" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">Hosting plans</code> menu, then select the web hosting plan concerned.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Multisite</code> tab.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Multisite" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/multisite.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    On the new page that opens, a table appears.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Multisite interface" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisite/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
    
    |Scenarios|Actions to Take|
    |---|---|
    |The domain name or subdomain linked to your website **appears** in the “multisite” table.|If you have just added your domain name or subdomain to the <code className="action">Multisite</code> section of your web hosting plan, please wait around **twenty minutes**, then refresh your web browser cache. If the message “Site not installed” still appears, go to [part 2](#check-dns-domain).|
    |The domain or subdomain linked to your website **does not appear** in the “multisite” table.|Add your domain or subdomain to the <code className="action">Multisite</code> section by following the dedicated section in the guide “[Hosting multiple websites on your web hosting plan - adding a domain or subdomain](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisites-configure-multisite/)”.|
    |The domain or subdomain **has been removed** from the "multisite" table without any action on your part.|Your domain or its DNS zone may be managed from another account. Add your domain or subdomain in the <code className="action">Multisite</code> section by following the dedicated section of the guide “[Hosting multiple websites on your Web Hosting plan - adding an external domain name](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisites-configure-multisite/)”.|
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


### 2 - Check the IP pointing in your domain name’s active DNS zone <a name="check-dns-domain"></a>

This step involves checking that your domain or subdomain points to your Web Hosting plan’s IP address from its active DNS zone.

:::info
To find out more about DNS, visit the following pages:

- [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit/)
- [Creating an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-create/)
- [Modify an OVHcloud domain name’s DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit/)

:::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>

#### 2\.2 Verify the IP address recorded in your domain name’s active DNS zone

You will now need to check that your Web Hosting plan’s IP address appears in your domain name’s active DNS zone.

:::info
Before you continue, whenever a change occurs in a domain name’s active **DNS zone**, you may need to wait between **4 and 24 hours** to update the information on the DNS network.

If you modify the **DNS servers** associated with your domain name directly, this period can be up to **48 hours** maximum.

:::


To do this, click on the tabs below to display each of the **4** steps in succession.

  <Tab label="Step 1">

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">

    If your domain name does not appear in the list that appears, it means that its DNS zone is not managed from your OVHcloud Control Panel.<br/>

Determine its registrar and the DNS servers associated with it via our [WHOIS](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/domains/whois/) tool.<br/>

Find and modify the DNS zone concerned accordingly by following the dedicated section of the guide “[Hosting multiple websites on your Web Hosting plan - adding an external domain name](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisites-configure-multisite/)”.

This means that your domain name is not managed from the OVHcloud Control Panel.<br/> Determine its registrar and the DNS servers associated with it using our [WHOIS](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/domains/whois/) tool.<br/> Find and modify the DNS zone concerned accordingly by following the dedicated section of the guide “[Hosting multiple websites on your website - adding an external domain name](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisites-configure-multisite/)”.
