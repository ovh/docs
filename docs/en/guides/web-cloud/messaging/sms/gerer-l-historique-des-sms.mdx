## Objective

You can view and download a log of your sent SMS messages from your OVHcloud Control Panel. This guide will explain how to do this.

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud SMS account with at least 1 sent SMS.

- **Direct link:** [SMS](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/telecom/sms)
- **Navigation path:** <code className="action">Telecom</code> > <code className="action">SMS</code> > Select your SMS account

## Instructions

The log records the date, time, sender, recipient and contents of the sent SMS.

:::info
In the OVHcloud Control Panel, you can view SMS messages sent in the last 6 months (or the last 5,000 SMS messages if you have sent more than 5,000 SMS messages in the last 6 months).

To view older SMS messages (up to the last 12 months), you will need to download your SMS history in CSV format. See [Step 2 of this guide](#csv).

:::


### Step 1: Viewing the log in your Control Panel

In the tab bar, click <code className="action">Message and campaign</code> then <code className="action">SMS Management</code> to access your unit SMS history or <code className="action">Campaign Management</code> to access your SMS campaign history.

Then click on <code className="action">Sending log</code> or <code className="action">Statistics and history</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory1.png" loading="lazy" />

You can click on the ‘Date’ column on the left to sort your log by send date.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory2.png" loading="lazy" />

The Actions <code className="action">...</code> heading at the end of each SMS allows you to view or delete the message.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory3.png" loading="lazy" />

To delete several SMS messages at once, tick the boxes next to each message. The <code className="action">Delete the SMS selected</code> button will appear above the log.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory4.png" loading="lazy" />

The <code className="action">Filter</code> button allows you to filter your search by sender (if you have several senders) or by recipient.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory5.png" loading="lazy" />
 
### Step 2: Downloading the sending log as a CSV <a name="csv"></a> 

Click on the <code className="action">Actions</code> button above your log then on <code className="action">Download</code> to download your SMS log in a .csv format. 
 
<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory6.png" loading="lazy" />

You can then view the log using a spreadsheet program. The information will display as in the example below.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms-history" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory7.png" loading="lazy" />

Here is a breakdown of the information included in the log:

|  Title  |  Description  |
|  :-----          |  :-----          |
|  id |  the unique id for the sent SMS used by our servers |
|  date | the date and time that the SMS was sent  |
|  sender |  the sender of the SMS message |
|  receiver |  the recipient’s phone number |
|  ptt |  the return code on the SMS status |
|  operatorCode |  the network ID of the mobile operator to which we sent the SMS |
|  descriptionDlr |  the description of the ptt code received and therefore the status of the SMS |
|  tag |  the tag attributed either manually via the APIs (to one or more SMS messages) or automatically by our servers to each sent SMS (or each SMS campaign) |
|  message |  the content of the SMS |

Further information about the ptt codes and various DLR IDs can be found in the final section of the guide [Everything you need to know about SMS users](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/tout-savoir-sur-les-utilisateurs-sms#step-5-specify-a-callback-url/).
