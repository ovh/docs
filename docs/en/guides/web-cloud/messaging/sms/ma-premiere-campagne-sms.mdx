## Objective

OVHcloud provides tools in the OVHcloud Control Panel for sending SMS messages. This guide helps you get familiar with these tools and send your first SMS campaign.

## Requirements

- an OVHcloud SMS account with SMS credits

- **Direct link:** [SMS](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/telecom/sms)
- **Navigation path:** <code className="action">Telecom</code> > <code className="action">SMS</code> > Select your SMS account

## Instructions

The toolbar and shortcuts will give you access to the main features for sending SMS campaigns.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms01.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1: Add contacts

#### Add a contact list

A contact list can be imported via a .csv or .txt file.

To add a contact list, click the <code className="action">Contacts</code> tab, and then click <code className="action">Create a contact list</code>. 

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms03.png" loading="lazy" />

With the following guide, you can find out how to [create a list of SMS recipients](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms/).

#### Add an address book

Unlike contact lists, address books contain contacts that you can name and more easily identify for targeted campaigns.

To add an address book, click the <code className="action">Contacts</code> tab, then <code className="action">Address book</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms04.png" loading="lazy" />

Refer to our guide on [Managing SMS address books](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-mes-carnets-dadresses-sms/) to find out more.

### Step 2: Create a sender

By default, SMS messages from an OVHcloud account in France are sent from a short number, which enables recipients to reply back. You may want to create an alpha-numeric sender name via the <code className="action">Senders</code> tab, so that your SMS messages are sent with your company or organisation’s name.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms05.png" loading="lazy" />

To do this, you can refer to the [section on choosing an SMS sender, in our guide on sending SMS messages via the OVHcloud Control Panel.](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/envoyer-des-sms-depuis-mon-espace-client#step-3-choose-an-sms-sender/)

### Step 3: Sending an SMS campaign

In the <code className="action">Message and Campaign</code> tab, you can access the sending options, the log of SMS messages sent and received, and scheduled SMS messages that are due to be sent in future.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms02.png" loading="lazy" />

To send a unit SMS message via the OVHcloud Control Panel, you can refer to the guide on [Sending SMS messages via the Control Panel](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/envoyer-des-sms-depuis-mon-espace-client/).

To send an SMS campaign, click <code className="action">Campaign Management</code>, then <code className="action">Create a campaign</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms06.png" loading="lazy" />

First, enter a name for your campaign in the field provided.

Then choose the sender from the list.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms07.png" loading="lazy" />

Then compose your message, and choose between sending it immediately or sending it on schedule. If you choose to schedule your campaign, you will need to define the date and time of sending.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="firstsms" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/firstsms08.png" loading="lazy" />

You just need to click the <code className="action">Send</code> button to send or schedule your campaign.
