## Objective

In the OVHcloud Control Panel, you can retrieve your SMPP credentials, change your password, manage access to the service, and transfer SMS credits.

**Find out how to manage an SMS SMPP account settings from the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

:::info
We recommend that you read the [technical specifications of the OVHcloud SMPP solution](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-specification/).

:::


## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud SMS SMPP account](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/sms/api-sms/)

---

### OVHcloud Control Panel Access

- **Direct link:** [SMS](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/telecom/sms)
- **Navigation path:** <code className="action">Telecom</code> > <code className="action">SMS</code> > Select your SMS account

---

## Instructions

Select your SMPP account. Its name is different from other OVHcloud SMS accounts. It starts with `smpp-` instead of `sms-` for standard SMS accounts.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/smpp-account.png" loading="lazy" />

### Credentials

In the `General Information` box, you can find the login details required to use your service. Use the button to the right of each field to copy its contents.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/smpp-account-ID.png" loading="lazy" />

If you have forgotten your SMPP password, use the <code className="action">Generate a new password</code> button. A new password will then be sent to the contact email address for your OVHcloud account, and it will be displayed to you.<br/>

Click <code className="action">Send</code> to confirm this action.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/smpp-account-password.png" loading="lazy" />

### Access management

Click the <code className="action">Options</code> tab and then click <code className="action">SMPP Settings</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/smpp-acl0.png" loading="lazy" />

The authorised IP framework lists the IP addresses of your SMPP clients that are authorised to access the SMPP server.

Click the <code className="action">Add IP</code> button to add IP addresses to this list.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/smpp-acl1.png" loading="lazy" />
