## Objective

This Exchange collaborative feature enables the creation of email addresses dedicated to resources in your organisation, such as conference rooms and shared equipment. Using these resource accounts helps with organising events in a collaborative environment by providing availability checks and seamlessly integrating resources with your Exchange calendars.

**This guide explains the managing of resources using the OVHcloud Control Panel and the Outlook Web App (OWA).**

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Exchange solution](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/hosted-exchange/) already set up
- login credentials for the email account(s) accessing the resource

## Instructions

Click on <code className="action">More +</code> in the horizontal menu, then on <code className="action">Resources</code>.

### Step 1: Creating a resource

<img className="thumbnail" alt="panelresource" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step1.png" loading="lazy" />

Click on the button <code className="action">Add a resource account</code> to create your first resource. In the new window, fill in the fields:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="createresource" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step2.png" loading="lazy" />

|Name|Description|
|---|---|
|Resource email|Enter the address for the resource. Please note that you cannot choose an existing email address.|
|Resource name|The display name that appears in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB) and in [OVHcloud webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/) (OWA).|
|Capacity|You can define the maximum size of a resource (specifying for example the seating capacity of a room or the seats in a shared company vehicle).|
|Allow conflicts|If this box is ticked, you will be able to create overlapping calendar events involving the same resource.|
|Resource type|Choose the resource type: "Equipment" or "Room".|

Click <code className="action">Next</code> to proceed to the summary, then confirm the task by clicking <code className="action">Create</code>.

### Step 2: Utilising resources

Your resources can be managed from the table in the `Resources` tab. Click on <code className="action">...</code> to modify or delete a resource. Additionally, the menu item <code className="action">Manage delegations</code> will be displayed. With this option you will be able to delegate access in the same way as for an Exchange account. Please find the details in [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-delegation/).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="resourceview" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step3.png" loading="lazy" />

### Adding a resource calendar in OWA

:::info
Please also refer to our guide on [Sharing calendars in OWA](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/owa-calendar-sharing/).

:::


Log in to your Exchange account via the [OVHcloud webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/). Switch to the "Calendar" interface by clicking on the "app launcher" in the top left-hand corner and then selecting the <code className="action">Calendar</code> icon.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="addcalendar" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-calendars-step1.png" loading="lazy" />

In the top navigation bar, click on <code className="action">Add a calendar</code> and then on <code className="action">From directory</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="selectresource" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step4.png" loading="lazy" />

Start typing to display suggestions from your contacts, enter a full email address or use the search option via <code className="action">Search Directory</code>. However, the resource email address should be suggested at this point because it was automatically added to the global address list (GAL) at creation. Click on <code className="action">Open</code> to add the resource calendar to your calendar overview.

### Creating an event in OWA

To schedule an event, first click on <code className="action">New</code> in the top menu bar and select <code className="action">Calendar event</code>. In the new window you can set the details for your event and add your required equipment and the location by adding the corresponding resource(s).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="scheduling" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step5_1.png" loading="lazy" />

The event planner consists of three panes:

#### **Details**

- (1) Add a title for the event: this will be displayed in calendars.
- (2) Add a location or room: here you can choose from your resource accounts.
- (3) Start / End: define the duration of the event.
- (4) Repeat: choose a repetition cycle if appropriate (daily, same day every month, etc.).
- (5) Reminder: OWA will display a reminder window at the specified time.
- (6) Show as: choose a status for your availability calendar.
- (7) Add an email reminder: an option to send reminders via email to yourself or all attendees.

Type your invitation message into the editor below (8) and proceed to add attendees to your event.

If you attempt to add a resource that is already booked ("busy"), a message will appear and suggest to use the ["Scheduling assistant"](#schedule) (9) which provides a larger calendar overview of the chosen time frame.

#### **People**

Since a resource account is also a contact, you can add rooms and equipment in this pane, exactly as with other participants (10). Start typing to display suggestions from your contacts, enter a full email address or use the search option via <code className="action">Search Directory</code>. (A click on <code className="action">+</code> will open your contacts.)

Once you have finalised the scheduling by clicking <code className="action">Send</code> in the top menu bar, the resource account will send you a message to confirm that it is booked for your event. Tick the box "Request responses" if you require active confirmation from the invitees to auto-update your calendar.

#### **Schedule**

A calendar excerpt of your own events labelled **Schedule** will show up on the right-hand side as soon as you add a resource or person to the event. It provides a graphical preview of resource availability on the chosen day; you can set the time and duration of the event directly via mouse clicks and the selection menu on the top-right.

If needed, click on <code className="action">Scheduling assistant</code> in the **People** pane to open an even more detailed overview. This assistant is helpful for bigger events or if you have to manage conflicts, because it visualises the entire scheduling process. You can verify availability and adjust your planning by selecting locations and contacts, without leaving this interface.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="assistant" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step6.png" loading="lazy" />

### Resource response messages

After creating the event (clicking <code className="action">Send</code> in the top menu bar) Exchange will send out messages:

- Attendees will receive invitations (to update the involved calendars or only their own, depending on whether you chose "Request responses" before).

- You will receive a confirmation email by every chosen resource account (if the resource is available or if it is booked but you **did tick** "Allow conflicts" at creation).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="acceptmessage" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step7.png" loading="lazy" />

- You will receive a decline email by every chosen resource account (if the resource is unavailable and you **did not tick** "Allow conflicts" at creation).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="declinemessage" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-resources/exchange-resources-step8.png" loading="lazy" />
