## Objective <a name="objective"></a>

By creating a delegation, you can enable an email account user to manage their own features (e.g. changing passwords). These features depend on the type of delegation set up:

- Delegate **all of your email accounts** to one or more OVHcloud accounts. This type of delegation allows beneficiary customer accounts to manage filters, email auto-replies, redirections/aliases, and mailing lists.

- Delegate **one or more email accounts** and their filters to a single OVHcloud account. This type of delegation does not allow recipients to manage email auto-replies, redirections, or mailing lists. It also does not allow recipients to delete the relevant email account(s), import emails from another account or manage delegations.

**This guide explains how to delegate email accounts for your MX Plan solution.**

## Instructions <a name="oldmxplan"></a>

:::info
When you set up a delegation on an email account, it will appear in the relevant [Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB). However, only the modifications listed in the [Objective](#objective) section of this guide will be possible in this situation.

:::


Switch to the tab <code className="action">Emails</code> to view the list of email accounts in your MX Plan solution.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="delegation" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-delegation/mxplan-delegation-01.png" loading="lazy" />

### Delegating all of your email accounts to one or more OVHcloud accounts

This type of delegation allows its recipient to manage passwords, filters, email auto-replies, redirections/aliases, and mailing lists.

Click the button <code className="action">Manage the elements shared by all email addresses</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="delegation" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-delegation/mxplan-delegation-02.png" loading="lazy" />

This opens a new section. Click the <code className="action">+</code> button to the right of `Add a username`. Enter the OVHcloud NIC handle that will take advantage of this delegation, and confirm your choice.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="delegation" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-delegation/mxplan-delegation-03.png" loading="lazy" />

You can delegate the management of your MX Plan service to multiple OVHcloud customer accounts.

### Delegating one or more email accounts to a user

With this delegation, the user can change the password for the email account concerned, and manage its filters.

To the right of the email account you want to delegate, click on the <code className="action">...</code> button, then <code className="action">Delegation management</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="delegation" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-delegation/mxplan-delegation-04.png" loading="lazy" />

Enter the OVHcloud NIC handle that will benefit from this delegation, and confirm your choice.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="delegation" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-delegation/mxplan-delegation-05.png" loading="lazy" />

You can add multiple OVHcloud customer accounts to manage each email address.
