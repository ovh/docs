## Objective

MX Plan email accounts can be configured on compatible email clients. This allows you to use your email address from your preferred device. Thunderbird is a free and open-source email client.

**This guide explains how to set up your MX Plan email address on Thunderbird for Windows.**

## Requirements

- You are using an MX Plan email account. This applies to the following services:
	- [Web hosting](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/) (included in all offers)
	- [Free 100M hosting](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/domains/free-web-hosting/) (included with a domain name subscription)
	- A separately ordered MX Plan service
	- A [Zimbra Starter email account](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/zimbra-emails/)
- You have the Thunderbird software installed on your Windows device.
- You have the credentials for the email account you want to configure.

## Instructions

### Add the account

- **On the first launch of the application**: a configuration wizard appears and prompts you to enter your email address.

- **If an account is already configured on the application**:

    1. Click on the <code className="action">☰</code> menu in the top horizontal bar.
    2. Click on <code className="action">New Account</code>.
    3. Click on <code className="action">Email Address</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-win-01.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
It is necessary to note the value corresponding to your location (**EUROPE** or **AMERICA / ASIA-PACIFIC**).

:::


Follow the configuration steps by clicking successively on the **5** tabs below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    In the window that appears, enter the following 2 pieces of information:
    
     - Your full name (display name).
     - The email address to configure.
    
    Click on <code className="action">Continue</code> to complete the settings.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-mxplan-02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    When Thunderbird detects an OVHcloud domain name, an automatic configuration related to the MX Plan offer is proposed:
    
     - If the information is correct, click on <code className="action">Continue</code> and proceed to step 5.
     - Otherwise, click on <code className="action">MODIFY THE CONFIGURATION</code> to perform a manual configuration.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-ssl0-03.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Incoming server settings:
    
     - **Protocol**: IMAP
     - **EUROPE (incoming) Hostname**: imap.mail.ovh.net **or** ssl0.ovh.net
     - **AMERICA/ASIA-PACIFIC (incoming) Hostname**: imap.mail.ovh.ca
     - **Port**: 993
     - **Connection security**: SSL/TLS
     - **Authentication method**: Normal password
     - **Username**: Your full email address
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-mxplan-04.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    Outgoing server settings:
    
     - **Protocol**: SMTP
     - **EUROPE (outgoing) Server**: smtp.mail.ovh.net **or** ssl0.ovh.net
     - **AMERICA/ASIA-PACIFIC (outgoing) Server**: smtp.mail.ovh.ca
     - **Port**: 587
     - **Connection security**: STARTTLS
     - **Authentication method**: Normal password
     - **Username**: Your full email address
    
    1\. Click on <code className="action">Test</code> to verify the entered settings.
    2\. Click on <code className="action">Continue</code> to validate these settings.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-mxplan-05.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 5">
    Enter the password associated with the email account, then click on <code className="action">Continue</code> to finalize the configuration.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-password-06.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


:::info
**POP configuration**

If you want a POP configuration for your email address, replace the settings in **Step 3** with the following:

Incoming server settings:

- **Protocol**: POP3
- **EUROPE (incoming) Hostname**: pop.mail.ovh.net **or** ssl0.ovh.net
- **AMERICA/ASIA-PACIFIC (incoming) Hostname**: pop.mail.ovh.ca
- **Port**: 995
- **Connection security**: SSL/TLS
- **Authentication method**: Normal password
- **Username**: Your full email address

:::

### Use the email address

Once your email address is configured, you can start using it! You can now send and receive emails.

OVHcloud also offers a web application to access your email account from a browser. To access the OVHcloud Webmail, click on [this link](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/). You can log in using your email account credentials.

### Retrieve a backup of your email account

If you need to perform an operation that could result in the loss of your email account data, we recommend making a backup beforehand. To do this, refer to the "**Export**" section in the "**Thunderbird**" part of our guide "[Manually Migrate Your Email Address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration/)".

### Modify existing settings

If your email account is already configured and you need to access the account settings to modify them:

1. Click on the <code className="action">☰</code> menu in the top horizontal bar.
2. Click on <code className="action">Account Settings</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-win-07.png" loading="lazy" />

- To modify settings related to the **reception** of your emails, click on <code className="action">Server Settings</code> in the left column under your email address.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-mxplan-win-08.png" loading="lazy" />

- To modify settings related to the **sending** of your emails, click on <code className="action">Outgoing Server (SMTP)</code> at the bottom of the left column.
- Click on the relevant email address in the list, then click on <code className="action">Edit</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="thunderbird" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/how-to-configure-thunderbird-windows/configuration-thunderbird-mxplan-win-09.png" loading="lazy" />
