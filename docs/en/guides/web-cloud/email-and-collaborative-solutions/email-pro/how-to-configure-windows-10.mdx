## Objective

The email addresses from the [Email Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/email-pro/) offer can be configured on a compatible email client. This allows you to send and receive messages from the application of your choice.

The **New Outlook** has replaced the **Mail** application on Windows since January 1, 2025. For more information on this topic, please refer to Microsoft's official page:  
[Outlook for Windows: The Future of Mail, Calendar, and People on Windows 11](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/office/outlook-for-windows-the-future-of-mail-calendar-and-people-on-windows-11-715fc27c-e0f4-4652-9174-47faa751b199)

**Learn how to configure your Email Pro account on the New Outlook for Windows.**

## Requirements

- An [Email Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/email-pro/) account
- The [New Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/office/getting-started-with-the-new-outlook-for-windows-656bb8d9-5a60-49b2-a98b-ba7822bc7627) for Windows
- The credentials for the email account you wish to configure

## Instructions

:::warning

To distinguish the two versions of Outlook when both are installed, type "Outlook" in the Windows search bar. You will then be able to see the difference as shown below. The new Outlook has no special mention.

### Add the account <a name="add-account"></a>

:::warning
In our example, we use the server name: pro?.mail.ovh.net. You will need to replace the "?" with the number corresponding to your Email Pro service server.

Click [this link](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/web/email_pro) to access the <code className="action">Email Pro</code> section. The server name is visible in the **Connection** section of the <code className="action">General information</code> tab.

:::


<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    - Open Outlook. In the left column, click on <code className="action">Add an account</code> to start the configuration.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="outlook" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/how-to-configure-windows-10/configuration-newoutlook-windows-01.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    - Enter your email address, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.
    - Enter your password and click the <code className="action">Show more</code> button.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="outlook" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/how-to-configure-windows-10/configuration-newoutlook-windows-02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    - Enter the following parameters:
       - **IMAP incoming server**: pro?.mail.ovh.net
       - **Port**: 993
       - **Secure connection type**: SSL/TLS
       - **SMTP username**: The email address you are adding.
       - **SMTP outgoing server**: pro?.mail.ovh.net
       - **Port**: 587
       - **Secure connection type**: STARTTLS
       - **Password**: Do not enter anything; the password entered earlier will be used.
    - Click <code className="action">Continue</code> to finalize the configuration.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="outlook" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/how-to-configure-windows-10/configuration-newoutlook-windows-emp-03.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


### Using the email address <a name="use-account"></a>

Once the email address is configured, you can start using it! You can now send and receive messages.

OVHcloud also provides a web application that allows you to access your email account from your web browser at [Webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/). You can log in using the credentials for your email account.

### Modifying existing settings <a name="modify-settings"></a>

The Outlook application does not allow you to modify the server settings for your email account.

If your email account is already configured and you wish to reconfigure it, you will need to delete it and recreate it:

- Click on the settings icon (&#9965;) at the bottom of the left column.
- In the "Your accounts" section, click on <code className="action">Manage</code> to the right of the relevant email account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="outlook" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/how-to-configure-windows-10/configuration-newoutlook-windows-04.png" loading="lazy" />

- Scroll to the bottom of the page.
- Click on <code className="action">Delete</code> to start the deletion process.
- Determine whether you want to delete it only from this device or from all devices using Outlook.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="outlook" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/how-to-configure-windows-10/configuration-newoutlook-windows-emp-05.png" loading="lazy" />

:::tip[Success]
Once your email account is deleted, follow the instructions in the "[Add the account](#add-account)" section of this documentation.

:::


### General sending and receiving settings <a name="settings-account"></a>

#### IMAP and POP receiving settings <a name="imap-pop"></a>

For receiving emails, we recommend using **IMAP** when choosing the account type. However, you can also select **POP**.

Select the tab corresponding to your configuration type:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="IMAP Configuration">
    - **Username**: Enter the **complete** email address.
    - **Password**: Enter the email account password.
    - **Incoming server**: pro?.mail.ovh.net (replace "?" with your server number).
    - **Port**: 993.
    - **Security type**: SSL/TLS.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="POP Configuration">
    - **Username**: Enter the **complete** email address.
    - **Password**: Enter the email account password.
    - **Incoming server**: pro?.mail.ovh.net (replace "?" with your server number).
    - **Port**: 995.
    - **Security type**: SSL/TLS.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


#### SMTP sending settings <a name="smtp"></a>

For sending emails, find the **SMTP** settings to use below:

**SMTP Configuration**

- **Username**: Enter the **complete** email address.
- **Password**: Enter the email account password.
- **Outgoing server**: pro?.mail.ovh.net (replace "?" with your server number).
- **Port**: 587.
- **Security type**: STARTTLS.
