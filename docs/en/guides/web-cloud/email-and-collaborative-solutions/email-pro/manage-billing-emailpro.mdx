## Objective

The Email Pro service offers flexible management for account billing. This guide details how to configure it.

**Find out how to manage subscriptions for your Email Pro accounts.**

## Requirements

- An [Email Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/email-pro/) account

## Instructions

### Ordering additional accounts

Go to the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab, then click <code className="action">Order accounts</code> in the top right-hand corner.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="billing_emailpro" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/billing-emailpro-01.png" loading="lazy" />

### Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts <a name="periodicity"></a>

In the window for managing your Email Pro platform, click the <code className="action">Actions</code> button in the top right-hand corner, then <code className="action">Billing mode</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="billing_emailpro" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/billing-emailpro-02.png" loading="lazy" />

In the new window, the table allows you to manage the renewal period for each account. You can apply a single billing mode for all accounts by clicking **Monthly**, **Yearly**, or **None**. You can also do this by selecting modes for individual accounts.

- **Monthly**: You are billed for the account each month on the date listed in the **Expiry** column.
- **Yearly**: You are billed for 12 months on the date listed in the **Expiry** column.
- **None**: The account will be suspended, then deleted on the date listed in the **Expiry** column.

:::info
For simplified management, the billing for each Email Pro account is automatically aligned at the beginning of the month. To make this alignment, and move the subscription date if necessary, you will receive an invoice from your Email Pro account on a pro rata basis.

**Example**: An order placed on 08 January with a monthly subscription will be billed again on 08 February. The following invoice will cover the period between February 08 and March 1st.

:::


<img className="thumbnail" alt="billing_emailpro" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/billing-emailpro-03.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code> to access the summary of the requested changes, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to confirm your choice.

### Deleting accounts

To delete an account within your Email Pro service, you will need to cancel it by changing its renewal configuration to **None**. To do this, go to the step ["Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts"](#periodicity).

Once you have cancelled it, the account will be suspended on its expiry date. If you would like to delete the email address hosted by the account before its expiry date, you can reset it.

Go to the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab of your service, then click on the <code className="action">...</code> button next to the account concerned. Next, click <code className="action">Reset this account</code>. Once you have reset the account, it will remain empty until its expiry date.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="billing_emailpro" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/billing-emailpro-04.png" loading="lazy" />

### Cancelling the service

Once you have deleted your email accounts, you will need to delete the domain name(s) associated with your Email Pro platform before cancelling it. To do this, go to the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab. Click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the line for the domain name you want to delete, then click <code className="action">Delete this domain</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="billing_emailpro" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/billing-emailpro-del-dom.png" loading="lazy" />

In the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab of your service, click the <code className="action">Actions</code> button in the top right-hand corner, then <code className="action">Cancel</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="billing_emailpro" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/billing-emailpro-05.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
This action suspends and then deletes all of the Email Pro accounts on their expiry date.

:::
