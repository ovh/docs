## Objective

Zimbra email accounts can be configured on different compatible email software. This allows you to use your email address from your preferred device. Zimbra includes an online calendar feature, which can be synchronised from software compatible with the CalDAV protocol.

**Find out how to add a Zimbra calendar to an application via the CalDAV protocol.**

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud Zimbra email account
- An application supporting the CalDAV calendar protocol installed
- Login details for the email account on which to configure the calendar

## Instructions

### What is the CalDAV protocol?

CalDAV is a protocol for editing online calendars and tasks. Zimbra email addresses have calendars that use the CalDAV protocol.

CalDAV calendar configuration is similar to email account configuration and requires an application that supports this protocol.

### Share a calendar

:::warning
This section only concerns the [Zimbra Starter or Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/) offers, which include the calendar sharing feature.

:::


#### Public sharing in ICS format

:::info
The ICS file format used here is static: the file version corresponds to the moment when the user generates the link. This means that an event added after the link to the ICS file is generated will not be present in the file or in the calendar into which it is imported. There is no synchronisation.

:::


To generate an ICS file link, follow the steps below:

- Log in to your Zimbra email address via the [webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/).
- Go to the <code className="action">Calendar</code> tab.
- Right-click on the relevant calendar and click on <code className="action">Share...</code>.
- Click on the <code className="action">Make public</code> tab.
- Tick the box <code className="action">Generate a public link</code>, copy or open the link in a new tab and download the ICS file.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-webmail-01.png" loading="lazy" />

The ICS file you have downloaded can be imported into an existing or newly created calendar.

#### Sharing by email invitation

Unlike ICS file sharing, sharing by email invitation allows you to dynamically share a calendar with other email accounts from the same domain name. Events and actions on the shared calendar will be synchronised.

:::warning
Only email addresses from the same domain name can receive this type of sharing.

:::


To initiate a sharing on another email address:

- Log in to your Zimbra email account via the [webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/).
- Go to the <code className="action">Calendar</code> tab.
- Right-click on the relevant calendar and click on <code className="action">Share...</code>.
- Click on the <code className="action">Invite by email</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-webmail-02.png" loading="lazy" />

Follow the steps below to share a calendar with one or more email accounts:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Enter the email address with which you want to share the calendar.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-webmail-03.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Set the permissions of the email account on the calendar, then click on <code className="action">Add</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-webmail-04.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Repeat steps 1 and 2 to share the same calendar with other email accounts from the same domain name, then click on <code className="action">Save</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-webmail-05.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    An email is sent to each recipient of the sharing and allows them to accept or reject the shared calendar. It also indicates the rights granted on this calendar.

If you share your calendar with an email account from the same domain name using the Roundcube webmail (MX Plan offer) or OWA (Email Pro and Exchange offers), the recipient email address receives a link to access the Zimbra webmail, allowing them to create a "guest" account to view the calendar.

### Configuring the CalDAV calendar on compatible software

We have selected stable applications that are compatible with the CalDAV protocol.

- **For Windows**: Follow the [Add a calendar on Thunderbird](#thunderbird) section.
- **For macOS**: Follow the section [Add a calendar on macOS](#apple-macos) or [Add a calendar on Thunderbird](#thunderbird).
- **For Linux**: Follow the [Add a calendar on Thunderbird](#thunderbird) section.
- **For iPhone and iPad**: Follow the [Add a calendar on iOS and ipadOS](#apple-ios) section.
- **For Android**: Follow the guide [Zimbra - Configuring your email account on the Zimbra mobile application](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/mail-app-zimbra-for-android-ios/).

:::warning
Android devices do not currently offer native support for the CalDAV protocol, neither is there currently a stable third-party app that is able to sync Zimbra calendars for our offers.

Only the Zimbra app, based on its webmail, is able to view online calendars on an Android device.

:::


#### General settings for a CalDAV Zimbra calendar <a name="general-settings"></a>

If you are using a CalDAV-compatible application, here are the general settings you need to set up a CalDAV Zimbra calendar:

- **Server/Address/URL**: Enter the value `zimbra1.mail.ovh.net`. For some software applications, you will need to include the “https” protocol in the address. Enter the value “https://zimbra1.mail.ovh.net”.
- **Username**: Enter the full email address associated with the calendar.
- **Password**: Enter the password for the email account associated with the calendar.

#### Adding a calendar on Thunderbird <a name="thunderbird"></a>

:::info
[Mozilla Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net/) is available on Windows, macOS and Linux. The following installation steps have been carried out from macOS, but they apply the same way to Windows and Linux.

:::


Open Thunderbird and click on the "Agenda" icon in the left-hand column.

Follow the installation steps by clicking on the tabs below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    - Click <code className="action">New Calendar</code> at the bottom of the calendar column or right-click an existing calendar and select <code className="action">New Calendar</code> from the drop-down menu that appears.
    - Select `On the network`, then click <code className="action">Next</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-thunderbird01.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Enter calendar login information:
    
    - **Username**: Enter the full email address associated with the calendar.
    - **Address**: Enter the value `zimbra1.mail.ovh.net`.
    - **This address does not request a login ID**: leave this box unticked, you will be asked to enter the password associated with the email account entered above.
    - **Offline support**: You can leave this option checked.
    
    Click <code className="action">Find Calendars</code> to begin calendar synchronization. Enter the password for the email account associated with the username in the window that appears, and confirm your entry.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-thunderbird02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The window below will appear with the CalDAV elements present in a Zimbra email account. Tick the items you want to appear in the Thunderbird calendar and click <code className="action">Log in</code> to complete the configuration.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-thunderbird03.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


#### Adding a calendar on iOS and ipadOS <a name="apple-ios"></a>

:::warning
The configuration below was made from an iPhone. However, the procedure is the same from an iPad.

:::


To add a CalDAV calendar on the Apple `Calendar` app on your iPhone or iPad, follow the installation steps by clicking on the tabs below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <code className="action">Settings</code> of your iPhone or iPad. Find the <code className="action">Calendar</code> section by scrolling through the menu, or by entering "calendar" in the settings search bar.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-ios01.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Go to <code className="action">Calendar Accounts</code> then select <code className="action">Add Account</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-ios02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Choose <code className="action">Other</code>, then select <code className="action">Add a CalDAV account</code> in the "CALENDAR" section.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-ios03.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    Enter calendar login information:
    
    - **Server**: Enter the value `zimbra1.mail.ovh.net`.
    - **Username**: Enter the full email address associated with the calendar.
    - **Password**: Enter the password for the email account.
    - **Description**: Add a description to the calendar.
    
    Confirm by clicking the <code className="action">Next</code> button.
    
    Choose the `Calendar` and `Reminder` applications that will use the Zimbra calendar information.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-ios04.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


#### Adding a calendar on macOS <a name="apple-macos"></a>

To add a CalDAV calendar on the Apple "Calendar" app on your Mac, launch the app and follow the installation steps by clicking on the tabs below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Click <code className="action">Calendar</code> in the top menu bar, then <code className="action">Add Account</code>. Select `Add a CalDAV account`, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-macos01.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the configuration window, enter the following information:
    
    - **Account type**: Choose `Manual` from the dropdown menu.
    - **Username**: Enter the full email address associated with the calendar.
    - **Password**: Enter the password for the email account.
    - **Server address**: Enter the value `zimbra1.mail.ovh.net`.
    
    To finish, click <code className="action">Log in</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra_app" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-calendar-sync/zimbra-calendar-macos02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
