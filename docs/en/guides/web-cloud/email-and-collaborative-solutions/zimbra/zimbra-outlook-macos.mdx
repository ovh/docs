## Objective

:::info
This guide is aimed at customers with the email solution [Zimbra Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/zimbra-emails/). This service will be available in beta version from July 2025.

:::


Zimbra Pro accounts can be configured on a Mac using the EWS protocol (**E**xchange **W**eb **S**ervices). This allows you to configure all the collaborative features of your email address at once. The application [Outlook on macOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-outlook/id985367838?mt=12) is available on the Apple App Store.

**Find out how to configure your Zimbra Pro email address in Outlook for macOS via the EWS protocol.**

:::warning
OVHcloud provides services for which you are responsible with regard to their configuration and management. It is therefore your responsibility to ensure that they function correctly.

This guide is designed to help you with common tasks. OVHcloud cannot provide you with technical support in this regard. You can find more information in the "[Go further](#go-further)" section of this guide.

:::


## Requirements

- A [Zimbra Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/zimbra-emails/) account
- [Outlook on macOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-outlook/id985367838?mt=12) installed on your device
- The login details for the email account you would like to configure

## Instructions

### Add the <a name="add-account"></a> account

- **When you start the Outlook application for the first time**: A configuration wizard will appear, prompting you to enter the first email address you would like to add.

- **If an account is already set up on the Outlook application**:  
1. Click <code className="action">Tools</code> in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
2. Click <code className="action">Accounts</code>.
3. In the “Accounts” window that pops up, click on <code className="action">+</code> in the bottom left-hand corner, then click on <code className="action">New account</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="mail macOS" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-outlook-macos/outlook-macos-add-step00.png" loading="lazy" />

Follow the installation steps by clicking on the **3** tabs below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Enter your email address under “Email address”, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="mail macos" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-outlook-macos/outlook-macos-add-step01.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Two scenarios are possible:
    
    - **If the “IMAP/POP” window appears**: Click <code className="action">This is not a POP/IMAP account</code>, then choose <code className="action">Exchange</code> from the “Choose Provider” window.
    - **If you go directly to “Choose the provider”**: Choose <code className="action">Exchange</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="mail macos" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-outlook-macos/outlook-macos-add-step02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Check and complete the following information:
    
    - **Method**: Choose `User name and password`.
    - **Email address**: Enter your full email address.
    - **DOMAIN\User name or email address**: Enter your full email address.
    - **Password**: Enter the password associated with your email address.
    - **Server**: Enter "zimbra1.mail.ovh.net".
    
    To finalize the configuration, tap <code className="action">Add an account</code> and select the features you want to use on your Mac.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="mail macos" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-outlook-macos/outlook-macos-add-step03.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


:::warning
If you encounter a sending or receiving error after following the configuration steps above, please refer to the “[Modify existing settings](#modify-settings)” section of this guide.

:::



### Use the email address

Once you have configured your email address, you can start using it! You can now send and receive messages, and manage your calendars and tasks.

OVHcloud also offers a web application that allows you to access your email address from an internet browser. You can log in to the [OVHcloud webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/) using your email credentials. If you have any questions on how to use it, please read our guide on [Using Zimbra webmail](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-zimbra/).

### How do I modify existing settings? <a name="modify-settings"></a>

To modify the settings of an email account that has already been configured, follow the instructions below:

1. Click <code className="action">Tools</code> in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
1. Click <code className="action">Accounts</code>.
1. Select your account in the left column.
1. Click <code className="action">Advanced...</code> in the bottom right.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="outlook macos" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-outlook-macos/outlook-macos-modify-01.png" loading="lazy" />

Find the settings in **Step 3** of the [Add account](#add-account) chapter.

### How do I delete an email account? <a name="delete-account"></a>

1. Click <code className="action">Tools</code> in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
1. Click <code className="action">Accounts</code>.
1. Select your account in the left column.
1. Click <code className="action">-</code> in the bottom left-hand corner
