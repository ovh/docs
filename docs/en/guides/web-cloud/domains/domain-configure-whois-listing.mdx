## Objective

WHOIS, short for "Who is?", is a protocol that allows you to access different information related to a domain name.

For example, you can find the following information (non-exhaustive list):

- Contact details of the holder (subscriber), the administrator and the technical contact of the service.
- Indication of availability for purchase.
- Creation, expiry, last modification and renewal dates.
- Status (created, blocked, expired, etc.).
- etc.

When using your domain name, you may find that you need to show or hide certain information in WHOIS.

**Find out how to show or hide certain information contained in a domain name’s WHOIS in the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- A [domain name](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/domains/)

:::warning
Some registries (organizations that manage domain name extensions, such as *.com*, *.net*, etc.) do not authorize the masking or display of certain information contained in WHOIS.
Registries define the display rules and determine what information the WHOIS extension they manage should or should not contain.

Therefore, it is possible that despite your wishes in terms of display or masking in the WHOIS of your domain name, your request will not be taken into account.

:::


## Instructions

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the [Domain names](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/web-domains/domain) page, then choose the domain name concerned.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domain Names" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-names.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="**Step 2**">
    On the page that pops up, in the **Configuration** box, click the <code className="action">Managing data protection</code> button.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure Data protection" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/configure-data-protection.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    On the new page that pops up, you can choose to `Display` or `Hide` the information relating to the holder, administrator and technical contacts of the domain name, subject to the actions authorized by the registry managing the extension.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure listing in WHOIS" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/configure-data-protection/configure-data-protection-in-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you have made your choices, click the <code className="action">Confirm</code> button.
    
    Changes may take up to **48** hours to take effect. These changes will only be applied **if authorized by the registry managing your domain name extension**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
