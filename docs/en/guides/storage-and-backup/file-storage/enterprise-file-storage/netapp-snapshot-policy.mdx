## Objective
  
In this guide, we will provide an overview of how to manage your snapshot policies for OVHcloud Enterprise File Storage volumes.
Learn how to create a snapshot policy, apply it to your volume, modify and delete it using the OVHcloud Control Panel.

## Basics

A volume snapshot is a point-in-time, read-only copy of a volume.<br/>
Snapshots are created from an existing, operational volume. They cannot exist without it.<br/>
A snapshot policy allows you to automate the snapshot creation using different parameters. The policy can then be modified and deleted if you no longer need it.

## Instructions

Select your service from the list.

### Creating your snapshot policy

A snapshot policy allows you to automate snapshots by defining the creation frequency in minutes, hours, days, weeks, or months. 
It is also required to specify the number of copies you want to retain.

1\. From your OVHcloud Enterprise File Storage Control Panel, select the <code className="action">Snapshot policy</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SnapshotPolicy" src="/images/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-snapshot-policy/Snapshot_Policy_1.png" loading="lazy" />

2\. Set your snapshot policy name, a policy description and then use the button <code className="action">+ Add a new rule</code> to add one or more rules to the policy.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SnapshotPolicy" src="/images/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-snapshot-policy/Snapshot_Policy_2.png" loading="lazy" />

3\. Fill in the fields to specify the time of day, days of a month, week days and months for the snapshot creation. You also have to enter a prefix for the snapshots, needed for naming them.

You can find more detailed information for each value by clicking on the question mark icon. By expanding the <code className="action">Example</code> section you can view two sets of policy rules with an explanation of their outcome.

Click on the check mark button to add the new rule. Once all rules are added, click on <code className="action">Create a new snapshot policy</code>.

:::info
You need to specify a prefix and a duration in minutes. You can optionally enter the hours, days and months of execution to refine your scheduling.

:::


You cannot modify a snapshot policy after creating it. You will have to delete it and create another one if you need to apply new frequency parameters.  

### Applying a snapshot policy 

Once the snapshot policy is created, you can apply it to a volume.

1\. Go to the tab <code className="action">Volumes</code> of your capacity pool.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="ApplySnapshotPolicy" src="/images/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-snapshot-policy/Snapshot_Policy_3.png" loading="lazy" />

2\. Choose the volume you want the snapshot policy applied to from the list.

3\.	Go the <code className="action">Snapshots</code> section and in the <code className="action">Manage the snapshot policy</code> section, select the policy you want to apply.

4\.	Click on the button <code className="action">Apply the policy</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="ApplySnapshotPolicy" src="/images/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-snapshot-policy/Snapshot_Policy_4.png" loading="lazy" />

### Deleting a snapshot policy

:::warning
A snapshot policy attached to a volume cannot be deleted. If you want to delete a snapshot policy associated to one or more volumes, you will have to disassociate the snapshot policy first by associating another snapshot policy to these volumes.

:::


To delete a snapshot policy:

1\.	Go to the <code className="action">Snapshot policies</code> tab of your capacity pool.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DeleteSnapshotPolicy" src="/images/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-snapshot-policy/Snapshot_Policy_5.png" loading="lazy" />

2\.	Select the policy you want to delete.

3\.	Click on the <code className="action">Trash can</code> deletion button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DeleteSnapshotPolicy" src="/images/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-snapshot-policy/Snapshot_Policy_6.png" loading="lazy" />
