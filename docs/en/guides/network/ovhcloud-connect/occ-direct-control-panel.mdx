## Objective

With OVHcloud Connect, you can link your company network to your private OVHcloud vRack network, without creating a VPN tunnel through the internet. This will give you a quicker, more stable connection with guaranteed bandwidth. 

**This guide will show you how to set up the OVHcloud Connect Direct solution via the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

:::warning
To ensure correct operation of this service, you must be aware of the [technical capabilities and limitations of the OVHcloud Connect solution](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-limits/) and configure your network devices accordingly.

:::


- An [OVHcloud Connect Direct solution](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/ovhcloud-connect/)
- An OVHcloud [vRack](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/vrack/)
- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB)

## Instructions

Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB), go to the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> section and click on <code className="action">Network</code>. Next, open <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code> and click on your solution.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="ovhcloud connect selection" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/occ_01.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1: Associating a vRack

You will need to link a vRack to your solution first. Click the <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code> button and select an existing vRack from the drop-down menu. 

<img className="thumbnail" alt="vRack association" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/vrack01.png" loading="lazy" />

A message will confirm the vRack association.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="vRack association" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/vrack2.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 2: Adding a PoP configuration

:::warning
Changing the PoP configuration from L2 to L3, or vice versa, would require deleting the entire configuration. We advise you to think carefully about your configuration choice before going any further.

:::


:::info
For more details on the differences between levels L2 and L3, see our [FAQ](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/occdedicated-faq/).

:::


#### Configuration 

Once you have linked your vRack, two `PoP configuration` menus will appear. Click the <code className="action">Add a PoP configuration</code> button in the first menu, select L2 from the drop-down menu, then confirm. 

<img className="thumbnail" alt="adding PoP L2" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/pop2.png" loading="lazy" />

The L2 configuration will then be enabled in the `PoP configuration` menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="adding PoP L2" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l2pop2.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Configuration L3 

Once you have connected your vRack, click <code className="action">Add a PoP configuration</code> and select the L3 configuration from the drop-down menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="adding PoP L3" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/pop1.png" loading="lazy" />

You will then need to enter the following:

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| Customer ASN    | Your AS BGP number, which is configured on your router located in the PoP |
| OVHcloud ASN    | The OVHcloud AS number that will be configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the PoP |
| Subnetwork in /30    | A size /30 IPv4 block, used for the link between your router and the OVHcloud Connect router located in the PoP |

<img className="thumbnail" alt="adding PoP L3" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3pop1-1.png" loading="lazy" />

The `PoP configuration` menu will then appear.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="ajout PoP" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3pop2.png" loading="lazy" />

You can also add a second PoP L3 configuration via the second `PoP configuration` menu.

### Step 3: Adding an availability zone configuration

#### Configuration 

When your PoP configuration is active, click <code className="action">Add a configuration</code> under the `AZ configuration` menu. Select an availability zone from the drop-down menu and confirm.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add AZ" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l2dc1.png" loading="lazy" />

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add AZ" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l2dc1-1.png" loading="lazy" />

The AZ configuration will then begin.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add AZ" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l2dc1-2.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Configuration L3 

When your PoP configuration has been set, click <code className="action">Add a configuration</code> under the `AZ configuration` menu. 

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add AZ" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc0.png" loading="lazy" />

Select an availability zone from the drop-down menu, then enter the information required. 

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| OVHcloud ASN    | The OVHcloud AS number that will be configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the AZ. This number may be different from the ASN chosen for the PoP |
| A /28 subnetwork  |  A private subnetwork configured in your vRack in the selected AZ. This can be an IPv4 block of size /28 or higher |

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add AZ" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc1.png" loading="lazy" />

You can add additional availability zone configurations by clicking on the <code className="action">+ Add a configuration</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add AZ" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc1-1.png" loading="lazy" />

You must also add a routing configuration.

##### **Adding a routing configuration**

Click on the <code className="action">(...)</code> button on the desired availability zone then on <code className="action">Add routing configuration</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add routing configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc2.png" loading="lazy" />

Then choose the routing type between Static and BGP. 

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add routing configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc3.png" loading="lazy" />

If you choose the type BGP, then enter the required information.

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| Customer ASN    | Your AS BGP number, which is configured on your router located in the AZ |
| IP Neighbour    | IP address of the BGP neighbour of your router in the AZ. This address must be part of the subnetwork specified in the `AZ Configuration` section |

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add routing configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc5.png" loading="lazy" />

If you choose the Static type, enter the required information:

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| Network Focus    | A prefix using CIDR notation |
| Next hop    | An IP address acting as gateway in the subnetwork range |

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add routing configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc4.png" loading="lazy" />

You can add multiple routing configurations within the same availability zone. The configuration type (BGP or Static) chosen for your first routing configuration will then apply to the next configuration on the same availability zone.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="add routing configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/l3dc6.png" loading="lazy" />

### Deletion of vCenter resources

Each resource (PoP or AZ) can be deleted individually, but deleting a parent resource such as AZ or PoP will automatically delete all the subresources.

Recursive removal is slower than sequential removal of each resource.

:::info
If a AZ configuration is shared between two or more OVHcloud Connect services, removing the PoP configuration from a single OVHcloud Connect service will not affect the AZ resource.

:::


#### Deleting a routing Configuration

To delete a routing configuration, click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the routing configuration to delete, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="deleting routing configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/deleterouting.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Deleting a AZ configuration

To delete a AZ configuration, click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the AZ configuration to delete, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DC deletion" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/deletedc.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Deleting a AZ configuration will delete the related routing configurations.

:::


#### Deleting a PoP configuration

To delete a PoP configuration, click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the PoP configuration to delete, then click <code className="action">Delete configuration</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="deleting PoP configuration" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel/deletepop.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Deleting a PoP configuration will delete the related AZ and routing configurations.

:::
