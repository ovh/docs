## Objective

The **OVHcloud Load Balancer** operates by default as a proxy. It can also be configured to redirect client traffic to a third-party website. This functionality is essential for scenarios such as **domain name migration** or enforcing the **HTTPS version** of a website. This is known as **HTTP redirection**.

**This guide outlines the process for integrating your web services behind an OVHcloud Load Balancer utilizing HTTP redirections.**

## Requirements

- Have an [OVHcloud Load Balancer](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/load-balancer/) offer in your OVHcloud account.
- Have access to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB).
- Have access to the [OVHcloud API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/).

## Instructions

### Overview

An HTTP redirect looks like this:

```bash
HTTP/1.1 301 Moved Permanently
Location: http://www.example.org/
Content-Type: text/html
Content-Length: 174
```

Custom redirects must be formatted as `<scheme>://<net_loc>/<path>;<params>?<query>#<fragment>`. Only one redirect can be specified per frontend.

Custom redirects can be specified via the OVHcloud Control Panel or via the API, on a new or existing frontend.

### Adding a custom redirect from the OVHcloud Control Panel

You can define a custom redirect from the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB) in the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> section and then <code className="action">Load Balancer</code>.
This can be done either on a new frontend during its creation, or on an existing frontend.

#### Adding a new frontend

In the <code className="action">Frontends</code> section, click on the <code className="action">Add a frontend</code> button to create a new one.

In the frontend editing page, select the <code className="action">HTTP</code> or <code className="action">HTTPS</code> protocol.<br/>
Configure the information as usual. However, it is unnecessary to specify the <code className="action">Default backend</code>, as it will not be used.

In the advanced settings, fill in the <code className="action">HTTP redirect</code>.

#### Editing an existing frontend

In the <code className="action">Frontends</code> section, click on the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the relevant frontend and select <code className="action">Edit</code>.<br/>
Make sure the selected frontend is of the `HTTP` or `HTTPS` protocol. Complete the configuration if necessary.
However, it is unnecessary to specify the <code className="action">Default backend</code>, as it will not be used.

In the advanced settings, fill in the <code className="action">HTTP redirect</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration of a Frontend Redirect" src="/images/network/load-balancer/create-redirectlocation/add_redirectlocation.png" loading="lazy" />

Once the frontend is configured, click on <code className="action">Add</code> or <code className="action">Edit</code> depending on whether you are configuring a new frontend or an existing one.
Do not forget to deploy the configuration.

To do this, you can either:

- In the `Status` section of the <code className="action">Home</code> tab, click on the <code className="action">...</code> button of your Load Balancer and then click on <code className="action">Apply the configuration</code>.

- In the reminder banner informing you that the configuration is not applied, click on <code className="action">Apply the configuration</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Application of a Load Balancer Configuration" src="/images/network/load-balancer/create-redirectlocation/apply_configuration.png" loading="lazy" />

### Adding a custom redirect from the OVHcloud API

In the [OVHcloud API](https://eu.api.ovh.com/), redirects are specified in the redirectLocation string:

**Creating a new frontend**

<Api version="v1" section="/ipLoadbalancing" method="POST" route={"/ipLoadbalancing/\{serviceName\}/http/frontend"} />


|Parameter|Meaning|
|---|---|
|serviceName|Identifier of your Load Balancer service|
|port|Port(s) of the frontend|
|zone|Deployment zone of the frontend|
|redirectLocation|HTTP redirect URL|

**Updating an existing frontend**

<Api version="v1" section="/ipLoadbalancing" method="PUT" route={"/ipLoadbalancing/\{serviceName\}/http/frontend/\{frontendId\}"} />


|Parameter|Meaning|
|---|---|
|serviceName|Identifier of your Load Balancer service|
|frontendId|Identifier of the frontend to update|
|redirectLocation|HTTP redirect URL|

**Applying the changes**

<Api version="v1" section="/ipLoadbalancing" method="POST" route={"/ipLoadbalancing/\{serviceName\}/refresh"} />


|Parameter|Meaning|
|---|---|
|serviceName|Identifier of your Load Balancer service|
|zone|Deployment zone of the frontend|
