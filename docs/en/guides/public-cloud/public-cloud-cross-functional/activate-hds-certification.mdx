## Objective

To make your Public Cloud infrastructure and platforms compliant with [healthcare data hosting](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/certification-conformity/hds/) in France, they must necessarily be certified as a healthcare data hosting provider (HDS).

OVHcloud Public Cloud services are HDS certified for certain activities listed in the [certification reference](https://esante.gouv.fr/labels-certifications/hds/certification-des-hebergeurs-de-donnees-de-sante) of the French Digital Health Agency.

The list of certified OVHcloud products is available on the page “[OVHcloud products HDS certification](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/hds-certification/)”.

**Find out how to enable the option for hosting healthcare data on your Public Cloud project**

## Requirements

- You must have signed up to the [Business or Enterprise level of support](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/support-levels/) for your OVHcloud account.

## Instructions

### Enabling the HDS option when creating a new Public Cloud project

Create a new Public Cloud project.

If you have subscribed to the Business or Enterprise support level, you can then tick the `Healthcare data hosting and HDS certification for this project` box.

You will then have access to the special conditions for hosting healthcare data. Check the information and tick the box next to it. Click <code className="action">Continue</code> to finish creating your project. The services in this project will be HDS certified, in accordance with the contractual conditions.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="activate HDS new project" src="/images/public-cloud/public-cloud-cross-functional/activate-hds-certification/hds-new-project.png" loading="lazy" />

### Enabling the HDS option on an existing Public Cloud project

Select the Public Cloud project for which you would like the services to be HDS certified.

Click <code className="action">Settings</code>. If you have subscribed to the Business or Enterprise support level, you can then tick the `Healthcare data hosting and HDS certification for this project` box.

You will then have access to the special conditions for hosting healthcare data.

Review the information and check the box for accepting the contracts. Finally, click <code className="action">Update</code> to finalize the HDS certification process for your project, in accordance with the contractual conditions.
