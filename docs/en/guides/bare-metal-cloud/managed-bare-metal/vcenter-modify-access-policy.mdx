## Objective

To enhance security for your Managed Bare Metal infrastructure, you can restrict and manage the access to vCenter.

**This guide explains how to change the access policy for vCenter in the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- a [Managed Bare Metal infrastructure](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/managed-bare-metal/)
- access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB)

## Instructions

In your OVHcloud Control Panel, open the "Bare Metal Cloud" section, then select your service under <code className="action">Managed Bare Metal</code> in the left-hand navigation bar.

From the main page of the service, switch to the <code className="action">Security</code> tab, then click on <code className="action">Modify vCenter access policy</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Setting access policy" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/vcenter-modify-access-policy/modifypolicy-01.png" loading="lazy" />

In the new Window, choose "Open" or "Restricted" from the drop-down list and click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to apply your selection.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Setting access policy" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/vcenter-modify-access-policy/modifypolicy-02.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If you have set the access policy to "Restricted", follow up by consulting the guide on [Authorising IP addresses for vCenter access](/guides/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/vcenter-authorise-ip-access/).

:::
