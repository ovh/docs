## Objective

You can associate a name, first name, phone number, and email address with a vSphere user in your Managed Bare Metal. The email address allows validation by token.

**Learn how to associate an email address with your vSphere user.**

## Requirements

- a [Managed Bare Metal infrastructure](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/managed-bare-metal/)
- access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB)

## Instructions

In your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB) go to the section `Bare Metal Cloud`. Click on `Managed Bare Metal` in the service bar on the left and select your infrastructure.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="user vsphere" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/vsphere-edit-user/addMailOnUser01.png" loading="lazy" />

Go to the tab <code className="action">Users</code>, click on <code className="action">...</code> to the right of the user concerned and then click <code className="action">Edit</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="user vsphere" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/vsphere-edit-user/addMailOnUser02.png" loading="lazy" />

The following window appears:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="user vsphere" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/vsphere-edit-user/addMailOnUser03.png" loading="lazy" />

You can set your name, first name, phone number and email address.

This window also allows you to add editing rights to **IP**, **Additional IP** and the **Token validator** used to approve certain sensitive actions on infrastructures.

Click on <code className="action">Confirm</code> to validate the modifications.
