## Objective

In certain cases — like replacing a datastore or scaling it up to a higher capacity, for example — it may be useful to remove a datastore from your cluster.

**This guide will show you how to securely remove a datastore from your infrastructure.**

## Requirements

* an [OVHcloud Managed Bare Metal](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/managed-bare-metal/) solution
* access to the vSphere management interface

## Instructions

:::warning
Please note that you cannot remove the  **two 300 GB or 1.2 TB datastores** included in your pack. For security reasons, the removal request will also be blocked if you have virtual machines (VMs) on the datastore concerned (they will be listed in the confirmation window).

:::


To remove a datastore, right-click on the resource concerned. Then select <code className="action">OVHcloud</code>, and <code className="action">Remove this Storage</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Choice of datastore" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/delete-datastore/removedatastore01.png" loading="lazy" />

A confirmation window will pop up. Confirm by clicking <code className="action">Next</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Confirm removal" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/delete-datastore/removedatastore02.png" loading="lazy" />

The removal request will then be processed.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Removal confirmed" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/delete-datastore/removedatastore03.png" loading="lazy" />

You can also monitor the progress of the datastore removal in <code className="action">Recent Tasks</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Removal monitoring task" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/managed-bare-metal/delete-datastore/removedatastore04.png" loading="lazy" />
