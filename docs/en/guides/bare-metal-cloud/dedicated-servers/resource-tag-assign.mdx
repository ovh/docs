## Objective

Tags are labels that can be attributed to your resources, allowing you to organize and manage them more efficiently.

Each tag consists of two parts:

- **Key**: Represents an attribute or category.
- **Value**: Corresponds to the information associated with that key.

For example, you can categorize your resources by site, service, or even security level. Using tags can make it easier to search, organize your resources, manage associated costs, and apply policies with the desired granularity.

**This guide explains how to create, assign and delete tags for each dedicated server from the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- A [dedicated server](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/bare-metal/) in your OVHcloud account.

## Instructions

### Assign a tag to a dedicated server via the OVHcloud Control Panel



<img className="thumbnail" alt="General information" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/general_information.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Tags** box, click <code className="action">Add a tag</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a tag" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/add_a_tag.png" loading="lazy" />

You are automatically pointed to the `Tags` tab.

Click the <code className="action">Assign a tag</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Empty tag list" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/tag_list_empty.png" loading="lazy" />

In the window that pops up, click in the <code className="action">Key</code> field to open the dropdown menu, then select the key you want.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Assign a tag" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/assign_tag.png" loading="lazy" />

Next, click in the <code className="action">Value</code> field and select the appropriate value from the dropdown menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Assign a tag - populated" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/assign_tag_populated.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
**If you want to use a key or value that does not yet exist**, you can create it by entering it, then clicking <code className="action">Add your-value</code>, where "your-value" corresponds to the text you have entered.

:::


Finally, click the <code className="action">Add</code> button to create the tag, then click the <code className="action">Assign</code> button in the lower right-hand part of the window.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Tag added" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/tag_added.png" loading="lazy" />

You will receive a green confirmation message, then see the list of tags applied to the chosen server.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Tags - list with example" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/tag_list_with_example.png" loading="lazy" />

### Delete a tag on a dedicated server

To find the list of tags assigned to your server:

1. Log in to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://www.ovh.com/auth/?onsuccess=https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/&from=https://www.ovh.co.uk/&ovhSubsidiary=GB).
1. Go to the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> section.
1. Click <code className="action">Dedicated servers</code> and select your server from the list.
1. Go to the <code className="action">Tags</code> tab.

Click the <code className="action">...</code> button, to the right of the tag you want to remove from your server.
Then click <code className="action">Remove</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Tag list remove" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/tag_list_remove.png" loading="lazy" />

A confirmation window will appear. Click the <code className="action">Confirm</code> button to unassign the tag.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Remove tag" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/resource-tag-assign/remove_tag.png" loading="lazy" />
