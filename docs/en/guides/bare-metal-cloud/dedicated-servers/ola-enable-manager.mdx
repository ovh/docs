## Objective

OVHcloud Link Aggregation (OLA) technology is designed by our teams to increase your server’s availability, and boost the efficiency of your network connections. In just a few clicks, you can aggregate your network cards and make your network links redundant. This means that if one link goes down, traffic is automatically redirected to another available link.<br/>
Aggregation is based on IEEE 802.3ad, Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP) technology.

**This guide explains how to configure OLA in the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- A [dedicated server](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/bare-metal/) from the Advance, Scale, or High Grade ranges in your OVHcloud account
- An Operating System / Hypervisor that supports the 802.3ad aggregation protocol (LACP)

## Instructions

:::warning
OLA is configured on all network interfaces. They will form a “private aggregation”.

Following the implementation of OLA, the public IP will no longer be accessible.

:::


### Configuring OLA in the OVHcloud Control Panel

To start configuring OLA, open the <code className="action">Network interfaces</code> tab on your server's management page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="network interfaces" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/ola-enable-manager/network_interfaces2022.png" loading="lazy" />

In the tab <code className="action">Network interfaces</code> (1), click on the <code className="action">...</code> button (2) to the right of "Mode" in the **OLA: OVHcloud Link Aggregation** box. Next, click <code className="action">Configure private aggregation</code>  (2).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="interface select" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/ola-enable-manager/interface_select2021.png" loading="lazy" />

Make sure that both your interfaces, or interface groups, are selected and give the OLA a name. Click <code className="action">Confirm</code> when your checks are complete.

This may take a few minutes. Once it is complete, the next step is to configure the interfaces in your operating system via a NIC link or NIC team. For the method to use, refer to the following guides for the most popular operating systems:

- [How to Configure Your NIC for OVHcloud Link Aggregation in Debian 9 using ifupdown](/guides/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/ola-enable-debian9/).
- [How to Configure Your NIC for OVHcloud Link Aggregation in Windows Server 2019](/guides/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/ola-enable-w2k19/).
- [How to Configure Your NIC for OVHcloud Link Aggregation in SLES 15](/guides/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/ola-enable-sles15/).
- [How to configure Your NIC for OVHcloud Link Aggregation in Debian 12 or Ubuntu 24.04 using Netplan](/guides/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/lacp-enable-netplan/)

### Restoring OLA to default values

To restore OLA to the default values, click on the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of "Mode" in the **OLA: OVHcloud Link Aggregation** box. Then click <code className="action">Unconfigure private aggregation</code>. Click <code className="action">Confirm</code> in the popup menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="network interfaces" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/ola-enable-manager/default_settings2021.png" loading="lazy" />

This may take a few minutes.
