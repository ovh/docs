## Instructions <a name="payment_methods"></a>

Open the [My payment methods](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/billing/payment/method) page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="hubpayment" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/right-menu/my-payment-methods.png" loading="lazy" />

The page that opens contains a table listing the payment methods saved to your customer account. You can:

- Add a payment method.
- Change your default payment method.
- Modify your payment method description.
- Delete a payment method.

### Register a payment method

When you place your first order, you are asked to save a payment method to ensure that your service can be renewed via automatic payments.

This payment method is then used by default for all of your renewals and is offered to pay for new orders.

You can save other payment methods so that they are suggested when you place new orders and used by default for future payments.

You can save two different payment method types:

- Credit card
- PayPal® account

To do this, simply click <code className="action">Add a payment method</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="manage-payment-methods" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/managepaymentmethods2.png" loading="lazy" />

Choose the payment method you want to use:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="choose-payment-method-no-sepa" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/choose-payment-method-no-sepa.png" loading="lazy" />

Follow the steps for saving a payment method. In the first step, you are asked to check the option <code className="action">I want to choose this as my default payment method as soon as it is validated</code>, so that it is used for future orders and automatic payments.

#### Credit card

<img className="thumbnail" alt="credit-card-no-sepa" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/credit-card-no-sepa.png" loading="lazy" />

To register a new bank card, you will be redirected to our payment provider's secure interface. A small amount of money is deducted from your bank account to check your card details and their validity.<br/>
No amount will be charged and your credit card will be activated in a few minutes.

#### PayPal account

<img className="thumbnail" alt="paypal_no_sepa" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/paypal_no_sepa.png" loading="lazy" />

Select <code className="action">PayPal</code> as your payment method, then click on the button <code className="action">PayPal</code>. A popup window will then open to log in to your PayPal® account and register it as an authorized payment method with OVHcloud.

Your PayPal® account will be activated within a few minutes.

### Change your default payment method

Payments for your services’ renewal bills are always taken via your default payment method. If you would like to change it, you will need to add a new payment method in the OVHcloud Control Panel.

To do this, click on the <code className="action">...</code> to the right of the new payment method, then <code className="action">Make this the default payment method</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="manage-payment-methods" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/managepaymentmethods3.png" loading="lazy" />

> **How do I replace my default payment method with another one?**
>
> - Step 1: add the new payment method
> - Step 2: set the new payment method as the default payment method
> - Step 3: delete the old payment method
>

### Delete a payment method

If you no longer want to use one of your payment methods, you can delete it by clicking on the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of it. Then click <code className="action">Delete this payment method</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="manage-payment-methods" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/managepaymentmethods4.png" loading="lazy" />

If you would like to delete all of your payment methods, all of your services must be set to [manual renewal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-use-automatic-renewal#manual-renewal/).

#### Delete a payment method via the OVHcloud API

You can delete a payment method via the API by connecting to [https://eu.api.ovh.com/](https://eu.api.ovh.com/).

Start by getting the payment method’s ID:

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="GET" route={"/me/payment/method"} />

Then delete the payment method using the ID you obtained in the previous step:

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="DELETE" route={"/me/payment/method/\{paymentMethodId\}"} />


:::info
For more information, please refer to our guide [Getting started with the OVHcloud API](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps/).

If you experience any difficulties finding your payment methods via the OVHcloud API, use the <code className="action">Edit description</code> function by clicking on the button <code className="action">...</code> next to your payment method in the [Payment method](#payment_methods) section of the [My payment methods](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/billing/payment/method) page.

:::


### Prepaid account

#### What is the prepaid account?

The *prepaid account* is added to the [My payment methods](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/billing/payment/method) page as soon as it is created. It allows you to credit your customer account in advance and use these funds to pay for your orders and renewal invoices.

By crediting your account regularly, you will ensure that your services will never be interrupted for default on their [automatic renewal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-use-automatic-renewal#automatic-renewal/).

To do this, simply go to the `Payment methods` section in your Control Panel:

- Go to the [My payment methods](https://manager.eu.ovhcloud.com/#/billing/payment/method) page.
- Select the <code className="action">My Prepaid Account</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="prepaid-account" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/prepaid-account.png" loading="lazy" />

#### How does it work?

When you have services set to *automatic renewal*, your bill will be deducted from your prepaid account as soon as possible.

If you do not have enough funds, your account balance will be negative and will remain pending payment.

If you have a valid payment method saved in your customer account, this amount will be automatically deducted within 24 hours and your balance reset. This will not affect the status of your services.

However, if you have not saved a payment method, you will need to pay the balance within 7 days from your Control Panel to avoid any service interruptions.

If you do not have a registered payment method, we recommend setting an **alert threshold**, to ensure that you have sufficient funds for your next bills:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="warning_prepaid_account" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/warning_prepaid_account.png" loading="lazy" />

If the credit available on your prepaid account falls below the defined limit, you will be sent a notification email immediately.

#### How do I credit my prepaid account?

In the <code className="action">My prepaid account</code> tab, click on the <code className="action">Credit</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="credit-prepaid-account" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/credit-prepaid-account.png" loading="lazy" />

In the window that pops up, enter the amount you want to credit, click on <code className="action">Next</code>, then <code className="action">Order</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="order-prepaid-account" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/order-prepaid-account.png" loading="lazy" />

On the purchase order that appears, select the payment method of your choice and pay for your order.
