## Objective
You can view and download a log of your sent SMS messages from your OVHcloud Control Panel. This guide will explain how to do this.

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud SMS account with at least 1 sent SMS.
- You must be logged in to [OVHcloud Control Panel](/links/manager), in the `Telecom` section, then `SMS`.

![SMS Control Panel](/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/telecom/tpl-telecom-03-en-sms.png)

## Instructions

The log records the date, time, sender, recipient and contents of the sent SMS.

> [!info]
>
> In the OVHcloud Control Panel, you can view SMS messages sent in the last 6 months (or the last 5,000 SMS messages if you have sent more than 5,000 SMS messages in the last 6 months).
>
> To view older SMS messages (up to the last 12 months), you will need to download your SMS history in CSV format. See [Step 2 of this guide](#csv).
>

### Step 1: viewing the log in your Control Panel

Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](/links/manager), then select `Telecom`. Next, click `SMS` and select your SMS account.

In the tab bar, click `Message and campaign` then `SMS Management` to access your unit SMS history or `Campaign Management` to access your SMS campaign history.

Then click on `Sending log` or `Statistics and history`.

![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory1.png)

You can click on the 'Date' column on the left to sort your log by send date.

![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory2.png)

The Actions `...` heading at the end of each SMS allows you to view or delete the message.

![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory3.png)

To delete several SMS messages at once, tick the boxes next to each message. The `Delete the SMS selected` button will appear above the log.

![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory4.png)
 
The `Filter` button allows you to filter your search by sender (if you have several senders) or by recipient.

![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory5.png)
 
### Step 2: downloading the sending log as a CSV <a id="csv"></a>
 
Click on the `Actions` button above your log then on `Download` to download your SMS log in a .csv format. 
 
![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory6.png)
 
You can then view the log using a spreadsheet program. The information will be able to be displayed such as in the example below.

![sms-history](/images/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/gerer-l-historique-des-sms/smshistory7.png)

Here is a breakdown of the information included in the log:

|  Title  |  Description  |
|  :-----          |  :-----          |
|  id |  the unique id for the sent SMS used by our servers |
|  date | the date and time that the SMS was sent  |
|  sender |  the sender of the SMS message |
|  receiver |  the recipient's phone number |
|  ptt |  the return code on the SMS status |
|  operatorCode |  the network ID of the mobile operator to which we sent the SMS |
|  descriptionDlr |  the description of the ptt code received and therefore the status of the SMS |
|  tag |  the tag attributed either manually via the APIs (to one or more SMS messages) or automatically by our servers to each sent SMS (or each SMS campaign) |
|  message |  the content of the SMS |

Further information about the ptt codes and various DLR IDs can be found in the final section of the guide [Everything you need to know about SMS users](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/tout-savoir-sur-les-utilisateurs-sms#step-5-specify-a-callback-url).
