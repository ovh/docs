## Objective

**This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to log in to Public VCF as-a-Service.**

## Requirements

- A web browser
- The delivery email

## Instructions

### First login

Open your web browser and go to the Public VCF as-a-Service login page. Enter your organization name (provided in the account creation email).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="First page connection" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-logging/vcd-organization-connection.png" loading="lazy" />

You will now be prompted to enter your username and password in the appropriate fields (this information is provided in the creation email).

Click the <code className="action">Log in</code> button to access Public VCF as-a-Service.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Login connection" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-logging/vcd-login-connection.png" loading="lazy" />

You will be redirected to your organization’s dashboard, which we will describe in [this guide](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-getting-started/).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Dashboard" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-logging/vcd-dashboard-view.png" loading="lazy" />

### Change password

Once logged in, look for a link or button labeled **OVHcloud Organization Administrator Admin** or similar. This is located in the upper-right corner of the user interface.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Admin Button" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-logging/vcd-settings.png" loading="lazy" />

Select the <code className="action">Change Password</code> option from the drop-down menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Change Password Option" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-logging/vcd-change-password-option.png" loading="lazy" />

You will be prompted to enter your old password and your new password.

Fill in the fields and click <code className="action">SAVE</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Change password" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/hosted-private-cloud-powered-by-vmware/vcd-logging/vcd-change-password.png" loading="lazy" />

When you change your password, you should receive a confirmation email.
